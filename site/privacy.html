Privacy

VisaReady Pack is operated by Bernardo Carvalho, Portugal. Contact: bernardo.carvalho20@outlook.com.

Your trip data

Everything you type into the form is processed in your browser. It is not sent to us or to any server. To keep your progress, it is saved in your browser's local storage on your device; you can remove it with "Clear all saved input" or by clearing your browser data.

License check

When you enter a license key, your browser sends only that key to Lemon Squeezy to confirm it is valid. The key is then saved in your browser's local storage so the pack stays unlocked.

Payments

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, the merchant of record. They process your payment and billing details under their own privacy policy. We receive order details (such as your email) from Lemon Squeezy to deliver your license and handle support or refunds.

Fonts

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of the request.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for access to, correction of or deletion of the order data we hold. Write to bernardo.carvalho20@outlook.com.
