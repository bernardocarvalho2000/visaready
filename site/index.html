Questions

Is this legal advice?

No. VisaReady Pack formats the information you give it into a letter and an itinerary, and checks it for inconsistencies. We never create fake bookings or tickets; you need real reservations. Approval is decided only by the consulate.

Which consulates does it work for?

All 29 Schengen states. The check tells you which country's consulate to apply to, based on where you spend the longest time (or where you enter first, if equal).

Is my data stored?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your input is saved only in this browser's local storage so you don't lose it, and you can clear it at any time. The only request we make is to Lemon Squeezy to check your license key, and it contains only the key.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If the pack doesn't work for you, ask within 14 days of purchase at bernardo.carvalho20@outlook.com.

What language is the pack in?

English, which most consulates accept. Check your consulate's requirements before you apply.
